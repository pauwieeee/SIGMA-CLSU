Note on "0..1" for ScholarshipAgency->Scholarship:
  A scholarship doesn't always belong to an agency (e.g. an Institutional
  scholarship stands alone), so the agency side is optional (0..1), while a
  scholarship can only belong to ONE agency at most.

============================================================
IMPORTANT MODELING NOTE — why StudentScholarship is its own class
============================================================
Student and Scholarship are MANY-TO-MANY:
  - one student can hold several scholarships
  - one scholarship can cover many students
In UML, a many-to-many relationship that also needs its own data (here:
which academic year, which semester, and what status — Active / For Renewal
/ Documents Incomplete / Pending Verification) must become its own class
sitting BETWEEN the two, connected to both with a dashed line labeled
"association class" if your tool supports it. That class is
StudentScholarship. Don't draw a direct many-to-many line straight from
Student to Scholarship — route everything through StudentScholarship.

DuplicateFlag connects to StudentScholarship TWICE (scholarshipA and
scholarshipB) because a duplicate flag is always about a PAIR of scholarship
records the same student holds — e.g. "this student has an active record
under both CHED-TES and Iskolar ng LandBank."
